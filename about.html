About

Barter is in Nepal's bones

Villages across Nepal still exchange goods by mutual need. Barter Nepal gives that tradition a modern marketplace.

Our story

From a village market to your pocket

Our founders watched a cashless village market work perfectly — neighbours exchanging produce and labour by need. The thrift boom (second-hand shopping is growing fast in Kathmandu) told us the timing was right to digitise it.

Our values

What we stand for

  1. Waste is a design flaw

    Every item swapped is an item saved.

  2. Usefulness over price

    Value is what something does for you, not what it cost.

  3. Trust by design

    Verified members, honest photos, public meetups.

  4. Community first

    We're a marketplace, but we act like a neighbourhood.

BNBarter Nepal

Barter Nepal is a cash-free exchange marketplace for used goods, skills and services — built on Nepal's living barter tradition and the global thrift boom.
