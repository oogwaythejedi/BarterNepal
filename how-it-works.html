How It Works

The fairest marketplace in Nepal

No cash changes hands. Usefulness decides the deal.

The flow

From list to swap

  1. List

    Photo, honest condition, and what you'd like in return.

  2. Match

    Our engine suggests complementary swaps — the 'double coincidence' solved by software.

  3. Agree

    Chat to confirm condition, timing and meeting point.

  4. Swap

    Meet (or courier), exchange, and both confirm.

  5. Rate

    Trust scores update — the community polices itself.

Etiquette

The golden rules of barter

  • Photograph honestly

    Show the scratches. Nobody likes surprises at the meetup.

  • Show up on time

    Reliability is the currency here.

  • Value both sides

    A good swap leaves both people satisfied.

  • Meet safely

    Public places, daytime, bring a friend for big trades.
