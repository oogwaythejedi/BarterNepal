Why barter

Better than selling, easier than donating

  • No cash, no haggling

    Value is matched by usefulness, not money. Both sides win by definition.

  • Less waste

    Every swap keeps an item out of the landfill and in a home.

  • A real community

    Local meetups, verified members, and neighbours swapping with neighbours.

  • Free for everyone

    Listing and swapping are free. Premium matching is optional.

  • Trust scores

    Rated swaps build reputation — bad actors get locked out.

  • Built on tradition

    Nepal's villages have bartered for generations — we just added matching.

Fair questions

Barter, demystified

How do you decide a fair swap?

You and your match agree — but our value guide (based on second-hand prices) keeps expectations realistic.

What if the item is worse in person?

Meet at public spots, inspect before swapping, and use the in-app chat as your record. Bad actors lose trust scores.

Can I swap services too?

Yes — tutoring, tailoring, plumbing, photography: skills are listed alongside goods.

Is it really free?

Listing and swapping are free. Verified members can upgrade for priority matching and delivery coordination.

What about delivery?

Most swaps are in-person; we also connect you with budget couriers for cross-city trades.
